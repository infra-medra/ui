## Checkbox selection

`selectionMode="checkbox"` cascades: checking a node checks every selectable descendant too, and an
ancestor only joins `selection` once *all* of its own selectable children are checked. A branch
with only some descendants checked reads as indeterminate without being added to `selection` itself
— no separate tri-state bookkeeping, just that one rule. `selection`'s shape is unchanged from
`'multiple'` mode (`readonly OrgChartNode[]`): reading it back gives exactly the nodes whose
checkbox is fully — not partially — checked, parents included.
