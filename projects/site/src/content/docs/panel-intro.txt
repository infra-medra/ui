Panel is a titled container — a header row plus a body, optionally collapsible. `toggleable`
makes the whole header clickable (not just the chevron) and adds the expand/collapse button;
`[(collapsed)]` is a two-way `model()`, local UI state rather than a form value, so you can read
or drive it from outside just as easily as letting the user click the header themselves. There's
no expand/collapse animation for now — content toggles via the `hidden` attribute, staying in the
DOM either way so `aria-controls` always points at something real.
