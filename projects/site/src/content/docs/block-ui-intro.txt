BlockUI wraps arbitrary content and overlays a mask + spinner directly on top of it while
`blocked` is `true` — scoped to its own box, not a viewport-level overlay, so it stays put over
its own content as that content scrolls rather than escaping into a top-level stacking context the
way Dialog/Drawer do. The blocked content gets the native `inert` attribute rather than a manual
focus trap, which removes the whole subtree from focus, pointer interaction, and assistive-tech
exposure in one step. The mask defaults to a spinner, or you can replace it entirely with your own
projected `<ng-template>`.
