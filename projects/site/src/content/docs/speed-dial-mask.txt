## Mask and staggered reveal

`mask` dims the rest of the page with a backdrop while the dial is open, the same overlay affordance
Dialog/Drawer use, and clicking it closes the dial. `transitionDelay` (in ms, default `0`) staggers
each item's reveal — item `i` starts its transition `i * transitionDelay`ms after the dial opens,
instead of every item animating in lockstep.
