Toolbar is a header/action bar with three named projection slots — `<div medToolbarStart>`,
`medToolbarCenter`, `medToolbarEnd`. `start`/`end` are equal `flex-1` siblings around `center`,
the classic "holy grail" layout trick, so `center` sits at true visual center regardless of how
wide the other two are, without measuring anything in JS. The whole bar (and each slot on its own)
wraps on narrow viewports instead of overflowing. `role="toolbar"` is applied as a semantic
grouping label only — this doesn't implement the full WAI-ARIA APG roving-tabindex pattern, since
projected content can be anything; normal Tab order between items already covers keyboard access.
