ToggleButton is a labeled two-state button built on the WAI-ARIA "pressed button" pattern (`aria-pressed`) rather than the switch pattern ToggleSwitch uses — reach for it when the state reads better as a word pair, like "Public"/"Private", than as a bare on/off pill. `onLabel`/`offLabel` swap the button's visible text as it toggles, defaulting to "Yes"/"No", and `onIcon`/`offIcon` optionally add an icon next to that text for each state. It reuses Button's own `buttonVariants` styling directly — filled and primary while pressed, outlined and secondary while not — so a future restyle of Button applies here automatically instead of drifting out of sync. Being a real native `<button>` under the hood, Enter and Space activation come for free, with no extra keydown handling inside the component itself.
