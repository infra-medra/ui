## Rich title and subtitle

`<div medCardTitle>`/`<div medCardSubtitle>` are rich alternatives to the plain-string
`title`/`subtitle` inputs, for a heading that needs an icon, a Badge, or a link — same
unstyled, consumer-owns-the-markup slot convention as the header/footer slots, not a
`TemplateRef`. Projecting one suppresses its plain-string counterpart underneath it, so a rich
title never doubles up with the `title` input's own heading.
