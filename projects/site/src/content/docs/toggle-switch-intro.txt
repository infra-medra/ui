ToggleSwitch shares Checkbox's architecture: a real, visually-hidden native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">` drives every interaction state — checked, focus, hover, disabled — through `peer-*` Tailwind variants applied to two purely decorative siblings, the track and the thumb; neither one keeps its own copy of that state. `role="switch"` is the standard way to turn a checkbox into an iOS-style toggle: same element, same Space-to-toggle behavior a checkbox already has for free, only how assistive tech announces it changes, "on"/"off" instead of "checked"/"unchecked". It has no `label`, `onLabel`, or `offLabel` input of its own — a toggle with visible on/off text is ToggleButton's job, not this component's, so wrap it in your own `<label>` for an accessible name. `size`, `invalid`, and `disabled` behave the same way they do on Checkbox.
