## Home crumb, templates, and per-item options

`home` is a separate leading item (`BreadcrumbHomeItem` — same shape as a regular item but `label`
is optional, since a home crumb is usually icon-only) rather than something you fold into `items`
yourself; its separator only renders when both `home` and at least one visible item exist.
`itemTemplate` (`<ng-template #item let-item>`) replaces an item's icon+label content, not its
`<a>`/`<span>` wrapper — the link/`aria-current`/`aria-disabled` semantics stay under the
component's own control either way. Each item also accepts `target` (forwarded straight to the
anchor) and `visible` (default `true` — `false` hides the item and its separator entirely, useful
for a step that only sometimes applies without restructuring the array).
