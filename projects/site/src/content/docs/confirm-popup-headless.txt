## Fully custom body

`headlessTemplate` replaces the popup's entire body — icon, message, and the Accept/Reject
buttons — with your own content, wired through `{ $implicit: data, onAccept, onReject }`. This
adds `onAccept`/`onReject` to the context: this service returns a handle to subscribe to instead
of taking callbacks, so a headless template needs its own way to resolve the popup. `contentTemplate` is
the lighter-touch version — it only replaces the icon+message block, keeping the buttons.
