ImageCompare stacks two images and reveals the top one, `beforeSrc`, only up to `position` — a
percentage `model()` — via `clip-path: inset(...)` rather than resizing an element's `width`, so
dragging the divider never forces a layout reflow on every frame. The whole host is the drag
surface, not just the thin handle: pointer listeners live on the container itself, so clicking
anywhere on the image jumps the divider there immediately and dragging can continue without ever
landing exactly on the handle first. The handle itself (`role="slider"`) is what carries keyboard
support — Arrow keys move it by `step` (default `1`), Home/End jump to 0/100 — while the two
`<img>` elements underneath are `aria-hidden` and `draggable="false"`, since there's nothing for a
screen reader to usefully announce about two overlapping copies of the same photo. `class` sizes
the host directly (e.g. `h-64 w-full`) — there's no separate wrapper element to size instead.
