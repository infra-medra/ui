## Scrolling programmatically

`scrollTop(value)` sets the panel's scroll position from outside — grab a reference via
`viewChild(ScrollPanelComponent)` and call it (e.g. from a "jump to top/bottom" button, or after
appending new content). The value is clamped to the panel's actual scrollable range, so a value
past the bottom just lands at the bottom instead of erroring.
