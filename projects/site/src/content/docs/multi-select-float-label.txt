## Float Label

Wrap the field in `med-float-label` with `variant="over"` and give the label `medFloatLabelText`. The
floating label depends on an `<input>` sibling that reports an empty or filled state, and the
MultiSelect trigger is not an `<input>` (it is a `<div role="combobox">`), so the label stays floated
all the time instead of dropping into the field when it is empty.
