## Links and badges

`MenuItem` isn't limited to `command()` callbacks: setting `url` renders the item's label inside a
real `<a [href]>` — Ctrl/Cmd+click, middle-click-to-new-tab, and "copy link" all come free from the
browser — with `target` passed straight through to the anchor. `badge` renders a small trailing
label (a count, "New", etc.) next to the item's own label. Every item type across the menu family
(Menu, Menubar, MegaMenu, PanelMenu, TieredMenu, TabMenu) shares this same `MenuItem` shape.
