## Modal and header

`modal` (default `true`) controls the backdrop: a modal drawer dims the page behind it, traps
focus inside, and closes on a backdrop click; a non-modal one (`modal="false"`) has no backdrop
and closes on an outside click instead. `header` renders a title bar, and `closable` (default
`true`) adds a close button inside it — both are optional if your projected content brings its
own chrome.
