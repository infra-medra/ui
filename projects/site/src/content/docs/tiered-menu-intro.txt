TieredMenu has the same declared-once, imperatively-toggled shape as Menu —
`<med-tiered-menu #menu [items]="items" /><button (click)="menu.toggle($event.currentTarget)">` —
but items can nest to any depth via `TieredMenuItem.items`, opening as a sideways cascade of panels
instead of Menu's flat list or Menubar's single dropdown level. ArrowRight on an item with children
opens its submenu and moves focus inside; ArrowLeft collapses only the deepest open level and returns
focus to the item that opened it, per the ARIA APG submenu keyboard pattern. Selecting any leaf item,
anywhere in the cascade, closes every open level at once.
