## Vertical orientation and custom slots

`orientation="vertical"` stacks segments top-to-bottom instead of side-to-side — an explicit
`verticalHeight` (default `'12rem'`) sets the track's length, since a vertical track has no natural
size to derive it from. `min` (default `0`) shifts the percent formula to account for a non-zero
floor. `labelPosition` moves the legend before or after the track. Five named templates
(`#label`/`#meter`/`#start`/`#end`/`#icon`) let you replace the legend, a segment's own content, the
slots immediately before/after the track, or a legend item's icon — `#start`/`#end` shown here with
the full `value` array as context.
