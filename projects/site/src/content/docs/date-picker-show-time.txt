## Time

`showTime` (single mode only) adds an hour/minute row to the panel, and the committed `Date`
carries a real time-of-day instead of always midnight. `hourFormat` (`'12'`/`'24'`, default `'24'`)
governs a suffix appended to the displayed text — a separate formatting concern from `dateFormat`,
never typed or parsed back from free text, so the field becomes read-only whenever `showTime` is
on. Picking a day doesn't auto-close the panel — a "Done" button in the time row closes it once
the time is also dialed in.
