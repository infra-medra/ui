## Sizes and states

Three sizes (`sm`/`default`/`lg`), plus `indeterminate` and `invalid` as independent boolean
inputs — both can be combined with any size or with each other. `disabled` also works the plain
way (no forms wiring needed) if you just want a checkbox that can't be interacted with. `readonly`
keeps the box focusable and visible but never toggles from a click or keypress — unlike `disabled`,
there's no dimmed opacity.
