## Custom icon+message only

`contentTemplate` is the lighter-touch sibling of `headlessTemplate` — it replaces only the
icon+message block, keeping the built-in Accept/Reject buttons. Context: `{ $implicit: data }`
(the resolved `ConfirmPopupData`). Reach for this for richer message content (an icon that isn't
one of the built-in severities, multi-line text) without rebuilding the button row yourself the
way `headlessTemplate` requires.
