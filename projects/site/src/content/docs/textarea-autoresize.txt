## Auto Resize

`autoResize` grows the textarea's own height to fit its content instead of scrolling internally —
the manual drag handle is dropped in favor of `resize-none overflow-hidden` while it's on, since a
user dragging the handle would just get overwritten by the next auto-adjustment anyway. There's no
`minRows`/`maxRows` cap; a consumer that needs one can pair `autoResize` with a plain `max-h-*` and
`overflow-y-auto` on `class`.
