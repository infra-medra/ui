## Timing and touch

`showDelay`/`hideDelay` (milliseconds, default `0`/`150`) debounce how quickly the tooltip
appears and disappears — a short `hideDelay` keeps it from flickering shut while a pointer
briefly crosses a gap. `tooltipDisabled` turns the directive off entirely without removing it
from the template. On a touchscreen, a tap opens the tooltip and a second tap (on the trigger or
anywhere else) closes it — there's no `mouseleave` to hide it, so hover's dismiss logic doesn't
apply there.
