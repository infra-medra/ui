## Lazy content

Plain projected content (the "Overview" tab above) is instantiated the moment `<med-tabs>` itself
renders, whether or not that tab is ever opened. Wrap a tab's content in `<ng-template>` instead —
`<med-tab header="Reports"><ng-template>...</ng-template></med-tab>` — and it's only instantiated
the first time that tab is activated, then kept alive (not re-created) on every switch back to it.
There's no separate `lazy` input: the `<ng-template>` wrapper is itself the opt-in.
