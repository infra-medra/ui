## Fully custom body

`headlessTemplate` replaces the dialog's entire body — icon, message, and the Accept/Reject
buttons — with your own content. Grab a `TemplateRef` from your own component and pass it to
`confirm()`; the context (`{ $implicit: data, onAccept, onReject }`) gives you the resolved
`ConfirmDialogData` plus callbacks that resolve the dialog the same way the built-in buttons
would. `messageTemplate` is the lighter-touch version — it only replaces the message text,
keeping the icon and buttons as-is.
