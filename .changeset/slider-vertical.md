`med-slider` now renders a real vertical slider with `orientation="vertical"`: the track runs from `min` at the bottom to `max` at the top, thumbs and fill follow it, and pointer presses and drags map the vertical position. The track defaults to `h-56`, which `class` overrides. The slider style recipes gain an `orientation` variant (default `horizontal`).
