## Findings

### F-01 [minor] `e2e/site/tabs-docs.spec.ts` omits real-browser keyboard navigation on custom-header tabs for `TBD-002.S1`

**Where:** `e2e/site/tabs-docs.spec.ts:15-16,127-158` · `TBD-002.S1`, Constitution Article XI
**Problem:** `e2e/site/tabs-docs.spec.ts:15-16,127` carries the `TBD-002.S1` tag (and is the coverage location cited by `medra coverage`), and `Scenario TBD-002.S1` specifies that when rendered in the DOM and a real browser, `clicking/keyboard-navigating between tabs switches aria-selected and visible panels` and `eager and lazy panel contents render only in their role="tabpanel" when active`. Lines `127-158` verify custom `#header` markup inside each `role="tab"`, initial eager (`templatePanels.nth(0)`) visibility and lazy (`templatePanels.nth(2)`) non-instantiation, and click switching across all three tabs, but omit keyboard-navigation (`ArrowRight`/`ArrowLeft`) assertions in Playwright and do not assert `templatePanels.nth(1)` is hidden before tab 1 is clicked. Because Jest `projects/medra/ui/src/lib/tabs/tabs.component.spec.ts:448-459` already covers `ArrowRight`/`ArrowLeft` keyboard switching with custom `#header` tabs and `TabsComponent.onKeydown` was unchanged, the behavior has compliance evidence and stays `minor` under Article XI.
**Fix:** In `e2e/site/tabs-docs.spec.ts:146-158` (with checkpoint consent if locked), assert `await expect(templatePanels.nth(1)).toBeHidden()` before clicking tab 1 and exercise `ArrowRight`/`ArrowLeft` key presses on the focused custom-header `role="tab"` buttons while asserting `aria-selected` and visible `role="tabpanel"` updates.

### F-02 [minor] `tabs.component.spec.ts` binds `activeIndex` from host and splits `TBD-002.S1` clauses across two `it()` blocks

**Where:** `projects/medra/ui/src/lib/tabs/tabs.component.spec.ts:374,395-397,410-460` · `TBD-002.S1`, Constitution Article XI
**Problem:** `CustomHeaderHostComponent` (`tabs.component.spec.ts:374,395-397`) binds `<med-tabs [(activeIndex)]="activeIndex">` with `activeIndex = 0` rather than leaving `activeIndex` unset at `TabsComponent`'s `model(0)` default (tested unset in `projects/site/src/app/pages/docs/components/demos/tabs-template.demo.html:1`), and the two `it('TBD-002.S1: ...')` tests (`lines 410-430` and `lines 432-460`) each assert only part of `TBD-002.S1` (initial `#header` + eager/lazy isolation in the first; click/keyboard switching + lazy activation in the second) rather than each tagged `it()` asserting every clause on its own.
**Fix:** In `projects/medra/ui/src/lib/tabs/tabs.component.spec.ts:374,410-460` (with checkpoint consent if locked), leave `activeIndex` unset on `CustomHeaderHostComponent` when testing the default active tab (or add an unset-default assertion) and consolidate or mirror the `#header` and interaction assertions so each `it` tagged `TBD-002.S1` covers all scenario clauses directly.
